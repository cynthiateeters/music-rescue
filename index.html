Music Rescue: turn a page of sheet music into an editable score

Choose a picture of one page of sheet music. Music Rescue reads the notes and gives you a MusicXML file you can open in MuseScore, Flat or Noteflight, and shows the result as notation beside the original so you can check it.

Your picture stays on your computer. The reading happens in this browser tab, and nothing is uploaded. It's built for one instrument per page, a melody or a piano part. In testing, pages with several instruments came out as a single line.
